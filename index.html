Pinyin Ear Trainer

Ten questions on the sounds English speakers mix up most: x / sh, q / ch, j / zh.

You'll hear one Mandarin syllable at a time. Decide which initial you heard. The character and answer are revealed after each pick. Questions are reshuffled every run.

The short version: x, q and j are made with the tongue flat and the tip behind your lower teeth — thin and bright. sh, ch and zh are made with the tongue tip curled back toward the roof of the mouth — dark and hollow, like an American "r" is lurking in there.
